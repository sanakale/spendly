Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026  ·  Last updated: 28 May 2026

At Spendly, your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly you agree to the practices described here. Please also read our Terms & Conditions.

01

Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense records — amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and timestamps, collected in an anonymised form to improve the app.
  • Device & browser info — browser type, operating system, and IP address for security and fraud prevention.

We do not collect payment card details, bank credentials, or any government-issued identification.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Create and manage your Spendly account.
  • Display your expense history, summaries, and insights within the app.
  • Send transactional emails (e.g. password reset, account verification).
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorised access.
  • Improve the reliability and performance of the Service through anonymised analytics.

We will never use your personal expense data to serve you targeted advertising, nor will we sell your data to third parties.

03

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured servers. We implement industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit (TLS), hashed passwords, and regular security audits.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from your profile settings. Deletion is irreversible and processed within 30 days.

04

Third-Party Services

Spendly may use trusted third-party providers to operate the Service, such as cloud hosting and email delivery. These providers are contractually obligated to handle your data only on our behalf and in accordance with this policy.

We use Google Fonts to serve typography assets. Google may collect basic request metadata (e.g. IP address) when your browser fetches these fonts. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

We do not integrate with advertising networks, social media trackers, or any analytics service that profiles individual users.

05

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out to us:

  • Email: privacy@spendly.app
  • Response time: within 5 business days

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
